# Kartify — what we do, how a shop owner starts, and how they run their store

This is the bot's knowledge. Everything the assistant tells a client comes from this file,
the owner's saved answers, and that client's own account data. Edit this file (or add notes in
Portal → Admin → WhatsApp bot → Business info) whenever something changes.

## In one line
Kartify (thekartify.com) gives a shop owner a ready online store — website, products, photos and
prices included — so they can sell online without buying stock, building a site, or shooting photos.

## Who it is for
- Shop owners selling clothing and footwear (retail shops, boutiques, resellers).
- People who want to start selling online but have no products, no website and no photos.
- Owners who already have a WordPress/WooCommerce site and only want the products.

## What the owner gets
- **A hosted online store** at `theirname.thekartify.com`, or on their own domain (we set up the DNS).
- **A ready catalogue**: we supply products from our verified sources — names, photos, sizes, brands,
  stock status and prices — and keep them updated automatically. The owner picks the categories.
- **Their own branding**: store name, logo, colours, banner, About and policy pages.
- **Their own prices**: the owner sets the markup, so our source price plus their margin is what the
  customer sees. The margin is theirs.
- **Orders on WhatsApp**: a customer checks out and the order reaches the owner; payment by UPI.
- **A dashboard**: orders, visitors, what people searched for and which products they opened.
- **WooCommerce plugin** instead, if they already have a WordPress site: the same products sync into it.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not hold stock for the owner. An order is shipped from our source after it is confirmed.
- We do not run their ads or handle their marketing.
- We do not guarantee any particular sales figure.
- Delivery dates, discounts, refunds and custom deals are decided by the owner (Pritesh), never by the bot.

## How someone starts (onboarding)
1. **Sign up** at app.thekartify.com — with email and password, or with a mobile number and OTP.
2. **Create the store**: choose a store name and the address (`name.thekartify.com`), add a logo and colours.
3. **Pick products**: choose the sources and the categories to sell (for example men's shoes, sneakers, watches).
4. **Set prices**: choose the markup bands — how much to add over the source price.
5. **Submit for review.** We check the store and approve it, usually the same day.
6. **Go live.** The store opens to the public at its address, ready to share on WhatsApp and Instagram.
7. Custom domain (optional): point the domain to us and we set up the certificate.

## Running the store day to day
- **Orders**: a customer orders on the store, and the owner sees it in Portal → Orders and on WhatsApp.
  The owner confirms the order, the payment comes by UPI, and the item ships from our source.
- **Adding or removing products**: change the selected categories, and the catalogue updates itself.
  Product details and stock come from the source; out-of-stock items disappear automatically.
- **Prices**: change the markup any time; the store's prices update everywhere.
- **Store look**: logo, banner, announcement bar, About and policy pages are all editable in the portal.
- **Analytics**: visitors, product views, searches and orders.
- **Before going live**: the store can be kept private with a preview password to share with friends.

## Plans
- **Free** — to try the platform.
- **Standard — ₹4,000 per month** — the full hosted store.
- **Search the product — ₹100 per month** — only the product search, for people who just want to
  look up products and prices, not run a store.
Billing is monthly. An invoice is raised before expiry and paid by UPI or the payment link in the portal.
The store keeps running while the plan is active.

## Common questions
**Do I need my own products or stock?** No. The catalogue and stock come from us. You sell, then we ship.

**Where do the product photos come from?** They come with the products, so there's nothing to shoot.

**How much can I earn?** You choose the markup, so the margin on every order is yours. What you earn
depends on how much you sell — we don't promise any figure.

**Do I need to know web design?** No. The store is ready; you only add your name, logo and colours.

**How long does it take to go live?** Usually the same day, once the store is submitted and approved.

**Can I use my own domain?** Yes. Point it to us and we set it up, with the certificate included.

**I already have a WordPress site.** Use our WooCommerce plugin — the same products sync into your site.

**How do customers pay?** Customers pay by UPI, and the order reaches you on WhatsApp so you can confirm it.

**Who ships the order?** We ship from our source once the order and payment are confirmed.

**What if the customer returns something?** Returns are handled case by case — the owner will confirm.

**Can I change categories later?** Yes, any time, and the catalogue updates on its own.

**Is there a contract?** It is monthly. Stop renewing and the store simply pauses.

**How do I cancel or pause?** Tell the owner, or let the plan expire.
