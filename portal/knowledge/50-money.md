# Money — plans, billing, shipping cost, the owner's margin

## Our plans — give the real number the moment they ask
| Plan | Price | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | ₹0 | Unlimited search & browsing · 50 product views · request new source sites · **no store** |
| Search the product | **₹99/month** | The above + **unlimited product views** · still **no store** |
| Standard | **₹2,499/month** | The above + **their own store/website** + **customisable layouts** |
| Pro | **₹3,999/month** | The above + **custom domain** + **their own payment gateway** |

- "I want my own store" → **Standard ₹2,499**. "I want my own domain / my own gateway" → **Pro ₹3,999**.
- ₹99 is catalogue browsing only — if someone quotes "₹99" thinking it includes a store, correct it
  kindly and tell them a store starts at ₹2,499.
- **Fresh store layouts every week**, included — they can restyle their store whenever they like.
- Shoppers pay by UPI inside the owner's store, or confirm on WhatsApp.
- Monthly. An invoice appears in the portal before expiry; paid by UPI or the payment link.
- **Grace period: 7 days** after expiry. If it stays unpaid after that, the store shuts down.
- **No pausing** for a month. **No refund** on a month already paid.
- **Plan changes mid-month are allowed** — the portal locks the plan once a store is submitted, so the
  owner just asks and we switch it.
- **No setup fee and no hidden charges.** Extra work is only charged if the owner asks for something extra.
- No GST number is needed to start, and we do not issue a GST invoice.

## What the customer pays, and what the owner earns
- The owner sets the markup. Source price + their markup = what the shopper pays, and the difference is theirs.
- **We do not cap or set their selling price** — that is entirely the owner's call.
- **The shipping charge is borne by the store owner** (out of their margin), not added to us.
- Orders are **prepaid only** today. COD is not available yet; it is planned.

## Getting their money
- How the money reaches the owner depends on their storefront plan — the owner (Pritesh) confirms
  the exact arrangement for that store. If a client asks specifically how they will be paid, hand it over.
- Once due, money reaches them in **2-3 days**.
- There is a minimum payout amount, but it is small.
- If a shopper never pays, the order simply stays pending — nothing is shipped and nothing is owed.

## Common questions
**"Kitna kamaunga?"** — The margin is whatever markup they set; we don't promise a figure.
**"Koi advance ya deposit?"** — No. Just the monthly plan.
**"Payment fail ho gaya"** — They can retry from the Billing screen, or send the UTR so we verify it manually.
**"Expire ho gaya, store band ho gaya?"** — 7 days grace; after that it stops. Pay and it comes back.
