# The product catalogue — searching, and adding a new supplier

## Searching (before signing in)
app.thekartify.com opens on **"Search the product catalogue"**. Type a product or brand in
**"Search by product name or brand…"**. Filters: Category, Stock ("In stock" by default), Sort
(Newest / price / name), Brand, Size, source site, and ₹ min–max. **"Clear filters"** resets them.

Each card shows the photo, brand, name, the supplier price and the available sizes. Tapping a card
opens the supplier's own page.

**Free views:** searching and filtering are always free. *Opening* a product is what counts —
3 opens without an account, 50 after signing up with a mobile number, unlimited on the ₹100 search plan.
Reopening the same product doesn't count again.

## Searching inside the portal
Left menu → **"Browse products"** ("Catalogue search"). Same filters, with labels: Search, Category,
Stock, Sort, Brand, Size, Source site, Price (₹). **No view limit here** — a signed-in client can
browse freely. Opening a product also refreshes its price and stock from the supplier.

## What they can sell — do not limit this to a few categories
Our catalogue spans many categories and grows every week. If a client sells something we don't
already stock, we add their supplier. That is the answer to "kya aapke paas X hai?" — ask which
supplier or site they buy from.

## Adding a new supplier (source)
Left menu → **"Request new source"**:
1. **"For which store?"** — pick their store, or leave it as "— not tied to a store —".
2. **"Site URL"** — the supplier's website address.
3. **"Category"** — type anything, for example perfumes, sunglasses, kurtis. It is not a fixed list.
4. **"Submit request"**.
The request shows as **Pending**, and we review and add it — **usually within a day**. Once approved
it attaches to their store automatically and the products start flowing in. The same thing can be done
from a plugin site under **"Sources & categories" → "Add a site" → "Not listed? Request new"**.

If the supplier is already in our system, it attaches instantly under **"Choose existing"** — no wait.

## Common questions
**"Aapke paas kaunse products hain?"** — Ask what they sell, then say we either have it or we add
their supplier within a day.
**"Mera supplier add ho jayega?"** — Yes. Send the site link and we check it; normally it's live in a day.
**"Photos kahan se aati hain?"** — They come with the products; nothing to shoot.
**"Stock khatam ho gaya to?"** — Out-of-stock items drop off the store automatically.
**"Price kaun decide karta hai?"** — The owner's markup over the supplier price. The margin is theirs.
