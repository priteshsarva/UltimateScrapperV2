# Signing up and getting started — click by click

## Signing up (app.thekartify.com)
The page that opens is the product search. Tap **"Sign in"** at the top right to get to the account screen.

**Fastest way — mobile OTP (no password):**
1. Tap **"📱 Continue with mobile OTP"**.
2. Enter the 10-digit mobile number (the "+91" is already there) → **"Send OTP"**.
3. Enter the 6-digit code → **"Verify & continue"**. The code is valid for 10 minutes.
   If it doesn't arrive, wait for the 45-second timer then tap **"Resend code"**, or **"Change number"**.
4. Then fill **"Your name"** and **"Email"** (a password is optional) → **"Save & continue"**.
   Name and email are required — invoices and order updates go there.

**With email and password:** on the OTP screen tap **"Sign up with email"**, then fill Name, Email,
Password (there's a **"↻ Generate a strong password"** link) and Mobile number → **"Create account"**.
Instagram, Facebook and WhatsApp fields are optional, under **"+ Add social & WhatsApp (optional)"**.

Already had an account with that number? Verifying the OTP signs you into the same account — it does
not create a duplicate.

## What they see on first login
**Home** with today's sales, total sales, orders and storefronts, plus a sales chart and their store list.
A welcome box asks what they'd like to do: **"🛍️ Create an online store"**, **"🔌 Use the WordPress plugin"**,
**"🔎 Browse the catalogue"** or **"➕ Request a new source"** — and walks them through it step by step.
There's also an offer: our team sets the whole store up for them for **₹499** ("Want us to set up your store?").

## The left menu
Home · My online store · Store layouts · Orders · Request new source · Browse products ·
WordPress plugin · Set up plugin · Wallet · Billing · Notifications.

## The path from signup to a live store
1. Sign up (above).
2. **"My online store"** → create the store: name, address, logo, colours.
3. Pick what to sell (sources and categories) and set the markup.
4. Submit it for review. We check and approve it — usually the same day.
5. It goes live at `name.thekartify.com`, ready to share.

## Sign-in problems
- **"Invalid credentials"** — wrong email or password. Use mobile OTP instead; it signs into the same account.
- **"Invalid or expired code"** — the OTP is older than 10 minutes. Ask for a new one.
- **"That email is already registered"** — they have an account; sign in rather than sign up.
- **"Account suspended"** — hand this to the owner.
