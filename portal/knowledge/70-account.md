# Account, billing screen and support

## Billing
Left menu → **"Billing"**: every invoice with its number, shop, amount, period, due date and status.
To pay an unpaid invoice:
- **"Pay now"** — opens the payment page; the invoice is marked paid once the payment goes through.
- **"Pay by UPI"** — shows a QR and the UPI ID. Pay, optionally type the **"UTR / reference number"**,
  then tap **"I've paid — send screenshot on WhatsApp"**. The invoice shows **Pending** until we
  confirm it, which we do after checking the payment.
After a card/gateway payment the screen confirms "Payment confirmed — your shop is active."

## Renewal and expiry
An invoice is raised before expiry. Reminders arrive by email and in Notifications
("needs renewal"). After expiry there is a **7-day grace period**; if it is still unpaid the store
pauses ("has been paused"). Paying the invoice brings it straight back.

## Notifications
Left menu → **"Notifications"**: new categories added to their sources, renewal reminders, store
paused, money released to their wallet, and shipment proof rejected.

## Account questions
**"Password bhool gaya"** — Sign in with mobile OTP instead; it opens the same account. Then set a new
password from the profile screen.
**"Mobile number badalna hai"** — Hand this to the owner; we change it from our side.
**"Email galat daal diya"** — Same, hand it to the owner.
**"Account delete karna hai"** — Hand it to the owner.
**"Staff ko access dena hai"** — One login per account today. Hand it to the owner if they insist.
