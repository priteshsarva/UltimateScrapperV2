# JD WebnShip & Selloship — what they are, and why an owner still needs us

Almost everyone we talk to already uses one of these. Never rubbish them: the person chose them, and
insulting their choice insults them. Respect the platform, then show what it does NOT do.
All of this was checked on their own sites — it is fact, not marketing.

## JD WebnShip (jdwebnship.com)
- Sells itself as "one stop ecommerce solution": a **free website** plus many courier partners.
- Pricing: ₹0 subscription, ₹0 website setup, 0% commission. They earn on the **courier/shipping** side.
- Gives: free store, Shopify connection, discounted shipping rates, live tracking, unlimited uploads.
- Their storefront (e.g. zenwatches.jdwebconnect.com):
  * Runs on **their** subdomain — the shopper sees jdwebconnect.com, not the seller's brand.
  * Product names come out as codes: "r@D0_AUTO_GOLD_TT_WHITE", "9ucc!_ROSE_BLACK", "RO_L3X._TIGER".
  * **Every** item shows the same fake MRP (₹7,499/₹7,500) against a ₹550-1,600 price.
  * **Checkout demands a phone login before anyone can buy** — "Login or Create account with your
    phone number". Every extra step loses buyers, and that buyer becomes the platform's contact.

## Selloship (selloship.com)
- It is really a **logistics company**, not a store builder: "reduce RTO by up to 96%", "deliver 5x
  faster", 99.6% delivery ratio, NDR management, **daily COD remittance**, monthly shipping plans.
- Free seller account; 5,000+ sellers claimed; support 10am-7pm Mon-Sat. Based in Surat, like us.
- Their storefronts run on cartpe.in (e.g. resellerzone.cartpe.in):
  * Shared subdomain again, and one we saw is literally titled "reseller zone".
  * Brand names deliberately misspelt: "Adidaas", "Nikke", "Tommy Hilfiggerrr", "Croccss", "Skecherrs".
  * Internal SKU numbers left in the title: "Adidaas Samba Og White Black For Her 477".
  * Same fake MRP on everything (₹10,000/₹11,000).
  * The page is blank for several seconds before products appear.

## THE BIG ONE — they don't give the seller a payment gateway
This is the strongest point we have, so lead with it, not with design or domains.
- On those platforms the store shows products, but the money does not land in the seller's account by
  itself. The order still ends in COD, a WhatsApp conversation, a screenshot, "paisa bhej dijiye" —
  and JD even makes the shopper log in with a phone number first.
- Every one of those steps is a buyer who drops off, and every order needs the seller to chase it.
- With us the shopper pays **inside the store**, by UPI, and the order is confirmed with the money
  already in. On **Pro (₹3,999)** the seller connects **their own payment gateway**, so it goes
  straight to their account, automatically.
- Ask it as a question, in their words: "Abhi customer paisa kaise bhejta hai — screenshot bhejta hai
  ya COD?" Then: "Wahi sabse bada farak hai ji — hamare store pe wo khud UPI se pay karke order karta hai."
- Say "no payment gateway for the seller", never "they are cheating" or "their platform is fake".

## Already have a WordPress / WooCommerce site? We have a plugin
- They do NOT have to move or rebuild. Our WooCommerce plugin pulls our whole catalogue into the
  site they already own — same products, their own prices, on their existing domain and design.
- This turns the commonest objection ("mera already store hai") into a yes: nothing is thrown away.
- Each connected site is its own subscription on the same plans, and it gets its own key.
- Setup is: download the plugin → install it in WordPress → paste the site's key → set margins → sync.
- If their existing site is NOT WordPress (cartpe, jdwebconnect, Shopify), the plugin doesn't apply —
  that is when the hosted store is the answer.

## What they do well (say this honestly)
- JD: shipping rates and courier choice, and it costs nothing.
- Selloship: RTO handling and daily COD remittance is genuinely useful for a cash-flow-tight seller.
- Both are fine ways to **start**. Many of our clients still ship through them.

## "But JD/Selloship is free, why pay you?"
This is THE question. Answer it calmly, never defensively:
- Free is the right price for a shipping funnel. On a free platform the store exists to move parcels
  through their couriers — that is their business, and it is a good one.
- What free does not buy: a name of their own, clean product names, honest prices, a checkout that
  doesn't push the buyer into someone else's login, or more than one supplier's catalogue.
- Money angle, using THEIR numbers: one or two extra orders a month covers ₹2,499. A shopper who
  abandons at a forced login costs them the whole order.
- Never claim they are cheating anyone, never promise we'll beat their shipping rates, and never say
  the words "scam" or "fake" about a competitor.
- They can keep shipping with JD or Selloship. We are the shopfront, not the courier — the two sit
  together happily.

## If they ask something about JD/Selloship we don't know
Say we don't know their internals and bring it back to what their own store would look like — then
offer the free demo in their name. Never invent a competitor's price, policy or feature.
