# Building the store — click by click

Portal → left menu → **"My online store"**.

## Creating it
1. Tap **"New storefront"** (top right).
2. **"Store name"** — required. **"Link"** is optional; leave it blank and it is made from the name.
3. Tap **"Create storefront"**. It is created as a **draft** and the guided setup opens immediately.
Another way in is **"Store layouts"**, where you pick a ready-made design and tap
**"Create store with this"**.

## The guided setup ("Setup steps")
Steps 1 and 2 are required; the rest can be filled later.

1. **Store basics** (panel says "Branding") — Store name (required), Logo URL, Favicon URL,
   WhatsApp number for checkout, contact email and phone. **Logo and images are pasted as web
   links (URLs), not uploaded.**
2. **Products** — tick the supplier sources that feed the store. Ticking a source brings in all of its
   products. At least one is required. Use the filter box to find a source. **Press "Save products".**
3. **Own payment gateway** — the owner's own UPI ID and payee name, so shoppers pay them directly.
   Leave blank for WhatsApp-only checkout.
4. **Colours** — four brand colours: Primary, Secondary, Complementary, Background. A warning appears
   if text would be hard to read on a colour.
5. **Homepage content** — hero style (full-width or split), announcement bar, hero title, hero image
   or video link, subtitle, About text, and customer review photos.
6. **Navigation & front page** — which categories show "In menu" and "On home", their order (▲ ▼),
   their labels and thumbnails, plus featured sub-categories and brands, and any custom links.
   **Press "Save navigation".**
7. **Homepage layout** — pick a ready design: Original, Atelier (fashion), Velocity (shoes),
   Chrono (watches), Redline (automotive), Haven (home). **"Preview"** shows it with the real products
   first; **"Use this layout"** applies it immediately. Tip: add products before choosing.
8. **Store info** — address, city, state, pincode, and Instagram/Facebook/YouTube/WhatsApp links.
9. **Policies** — shipping, returns, privacy and terms text, which become the store's policy pages.
10. **Pricing markup** — see below.
11. **Analytics pixels** — Google Analytics and Meta Pixel IDs, if they use them.
12. **Custom domain** — their own domain instead of the free address (see below).
13. **Submit & go live** — pick a plan, then **"Submit for approval"**.

## Pricing markup (how the selling price is decided)
Rows of **Min ₹ / Max ₹ / Markup ₹**. The markup is a **flat rupee amount added** to the supplier's
cost price in that band, not a percentage. Default bands:
₹0-500 → +₹750 · ₹500-4500 → +₹1000 · ₹4500-6000 → +₹1250 · ₹6000+ → +₹1500.
Edit any cell to use their own numbers, or **"Reset to platform default"** to go back.
That markup is the owner's margin.

## IMPORTANT — what people lose work on
On **Products**, **Navigation**, **Homepage layout** and **Custom domain**, the
"Save & continue →" button does NOT save. They must press that step's own button:
**"Save products"**, **"Save navigation"**, **"Use this layout"**, **"Save domain"**.
The other steps do save with "Save & continue →". If someone says their products or menu
"didn't save", this is almost always why.

## Going live
1. Finish at least Store basics and Products.
2. Go to **"Submit & go live"**, pick the plan, tap **"Submit for approval"**.
   Always submit from this step — the banner's "Submit for review" button doesn't ask for a plan,
   and the **plan cannot be changed once it leaves draft**.
3. Status becomes **Pending**: "Submitted — awaiting admin approval."
4. We approve → status **Approved** and an invoice appears → **"Pay now"**.
5. Paid → **Active**, live at their link, renewing monthly.

## The statuses
- **Draft** — only visible with the preview password. Still editable and deletable.
- **Pending** — waiting for our approval.
- **Approved** — approved; pay the invoice to go live.
- **Active** — live. The header shows "expires {date}".
- **Paused** — the renewal wasn't paid within the grace period; shoppers can't reach it. Paying brings it back.
- **Expired / Rejected** — contact us.

## Sharing before launch
A draft store has a **"Preview access"** row with the preview link and a password. Anyone given both
can view the store before it goes live. The password is generated by us and can't be changed.

## Custom domain
Step 12: type the domain (e.g. yourbrand.com) → **"Save domain"** → add the two DNS records shown at
the domain registrar (each has a **"Copy"** button) → **"Check status"**. HTTPS is set up
automatically, usually in a few minutes. No nameserver change is needed. On some plans this is locked
and shows "Custom domain — not in your plan".

## Editing later
The toggle at the top switches between **"Guided setup"** and **"Edit all settings"**, where every
panel is on one page. The full editor also has **"Category grouping"** (tidy up supplier category
names, one row at a time with "Map"/"Update"), **"Sales & visitors"** analytics,
**"Fulfilment & payments"**, and **"Orders"**.

## Things people ask
**"Store delete kaise karu?"** — The "Delete" button only shows before the store is live. A live store
has to be cancelled with us first.
**"Pause kaise karu?"** — There is no pause button. A store pauses by itself if the renewal isn't paid.
**"Paused ho gaya, wapas kaise aaye?"** — Pay the pending invoice from the Payment step.
**"Logo upload kaise karu?"** — Paste the image's web link in "Logo URL". Only review photos are uploaded.
**"Layout badal sakte hain?"** — Yes, any time, from "Homepage layout". Preview first.
**"Apna domain laga sakte hain?"** — Yes, step 12, with two DNS records.
